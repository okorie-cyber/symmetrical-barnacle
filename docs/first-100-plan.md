# First $100 (≈ ₦160,000) with ₦0 spent: 14-day plan

**Supersedes** `first-customer-plan.md` for now. The $497 US Fix Sprint stays on the menu, but it's no longer the first target.

**Why Lagos first:**
- Payment arrives by bank transfer at ₦0 cost the same day. Fiverr holds money for 14 days after completion; Upwork charges Connects.
- Trust builds faster face to face.
- Your network gives warm introductions.
- Fiverr gigs still go live in parallel to start collecting views.

## Funnel maths (assumptions to measure, not promises)
- 40 real conversations → about 8 interested → about 2–3 quotes → about 1 deposit
- **The first $100 needs about 40 conversations,** roughly 4 a day for 10 days.

## Day by day

| Day | You (≈2 hrs/day) | Me |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Approve the assets. Post the WhatsApp status (message 1). Send personal message 2 to 10 contacts who own or know businesses. | Personalise messages from details you give me; prepare a quote template |
| 2 | Find 15 businesses near you on Google Maps / Instagram with no website or a weak profile; log them in Notion | Draft a gap message (message 5) per business from what you find |
| 3–4 | Walk-in visits: 10 businesses around campus/Yaba/Akoka. Show them their Maps listing on your phone. | Prepare a one-page printable price sheet |
| 3 | Create the Fiverr seller profile (free) and publish Gigs 1 and 2 after approval | Polish gig copy and portfolio images |
| 5–7 | Follow up with everyone. Send samples. **Quote: ₦35k GBP / ₦90k landing page / ₦150k site.** | Draft every follow-up and quote |
| As soon as there's a "yes" | Collect the 50% deposit by bank transfer, then start | Build the site from the portfolio template within 5 days |
| 8–14 | Deliver. Ask for a testimonial and 2 referrals. Keep 4 conversations a day going. | Update Notion; draft the case study for the portfolio |

**Fallback if nothing by day 10:** switch to the fastest small sales:
- WhatsApp Business setup (₦25k)
- GBP setup (₦35k)
- Sheets tracker (₦25k)

Four or five of these also reach ₦160k.

## What we track (in Notion)
Conversations started, replies, quotes sent, deposits, revenue received (not quoted), hours spent.

## Reinvestment rule (updated per your instruction)
- **Zero startup capital** until revenue is consistent. Revenue first covers your own needs.
- **No automatic purchases.** No domain or subscription is bought just because money came in. Each expense must name the real opportunity it serves, the free alternative we tried, and the result we expect. You approve each one.
- **Clients always pay for their own** domains, hosting and third-party tools, directly to the provider.
