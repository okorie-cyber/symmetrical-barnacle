## 2. Competitors: emerging market or established players?

**Honest answer: both, depending on the layer.** Tracking software is established and cheap. Done-for-you service for single-location trades is fragmented, and most agencies have simply added "AI" to an existing SEO retainer.

### What this means for us

- We are **not first**, and we should never claim to be. Owners will hear "AI visibility" pitches from many sellers.
- Our wedge is narrow but real:
  1. **Evidence before purchase.** A free audit built from their own market's answers, labelled observed vs approximation.
  2. **A fixed, itemised deliverable list** instead of a vague retainer.
  3. **Month-to-month** terms.
  4. **A price** between DIY software and agencies.
- **Biggest threats:**
  - BrightLocal at $31 for owners who'll do it themselves
  - Footbridge at $249/mo flat
  - Established agencies with case studies and US phone presence
- **Our weaknesses:** no case studies, no reviews, operating from Nigeria (which we disclose honestly), no US phone number.
