# Daily Customer Acquisition Routine (≈ 75 minutes, built around lectures)

**The single number that matters:** new real conversations per day. **Target: 4.** About 40 conversations should produce the first paid job; that's an estimate to test, not a promise.

## Every weekday (75 min, any time that suits you)

| Minutes | Do | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Open Notion → Leads, filtered to Next follow-up = today. Send the due follow-ups (pre-approved templates). | Notion, WhatsApp |
| 30 | **Start 4 new conversations** from the approved batch: WhatsApp or Instagram messages, or calls. Personal touch: mention one real thing about their business. | WhatsApp, Instagram |
| 10 | Reply to anyone who answered. Interested? Ask 3 questions (what they sell most, who buys, how customers order), then tell me and I'll draft the quote. | WhatsApp |
| 10 | Log everything: Activity row per message/reply; update Stage and Next follow-up. | Notion |
| 15 | Find 5 new businesses to add tomorrow (Google Maps/Instagram: no website, an incomplete listing, or "DM to order"). Paste name + link into Notion. I write the personalised drafts. | Maps, Instagram, Notion |

## 3× a week (10 min)
Post one WhatsApp status or Instagram story: a sample site screenshot, a before/after of a Google listing, or a one-line tip. After the first client, post their (permitted) result.

## Saturday (2 hours, optional but powerful)
Walk-ins: 6–10 businesses around Akoka/Yaba with the walk-in script. Show them their own Google Maps listing on your phone.

## Sunday (20 min with me)
Weekly review: conversations started, replies, quotes, deposits, ₦ received, hours spent. We adjust the message or target type based on what got replies.

## My side (every day you check in)
- Draft personalised messages for the leads you added (you approve; you send)
- Draft quotes and invoices on request
- Build sites from the intake answers within 24–48 hours of a deposit
- Keep Notion consistent

## Weekly targets
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| New conversations | 20 |
| Follow-ups | 10 |
| Quotes sent | 2 |
| Deposits | 1 (from week 2 onward) |

**If two weeks pass with 40+ conversations and no deposit, we change one thing:** the business type, the offer (lead with the ₦25k–₦35k quick wins) or the message.
