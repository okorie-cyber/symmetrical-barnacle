# Financial Milestones to $1,000,000 (cumulative revenue)

**These are targets, not forecasts.** They rest on the assumptions below. If an assumption fails, the timeline moves. We update this file with real numbers every month.

## Assumptions

- Offer: $497 Fix Sprint (one-time) + $297/mo retainer. New clients pay $397 to $497/mo from about month 10, once we have case studies.
- Funnel (to be validated): 100 personalised emails → 3 to 5 positive replies → 1 to 2 Fix Sprints. 50% of Fix Sprint clients take the retainer.
- Retainer churn: 8% a month (average lifetime of about 12 months).
- Delivery: about 3.5 hours per retainer client a month, rising to about 1.5 hours with automation.
- Definitions: **Revenue** = money invoiced and paid. **Net profit** = revenue minus all expenses, including contractors and fees. **Cash flow** = timing of money in vs out (sprints arrive upfront, retainers monthly). **Personal wealth** = what you keep after tax, living costs and reinvestment, which is far below revenue.
