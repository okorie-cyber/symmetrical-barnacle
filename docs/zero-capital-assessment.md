# Zero-Capital Assessment: 15 services we can sell now

**Rule:** no money is spent before revenue. Anything that costs money is either paid for by the client (their domain, their software accounts), done manually, or left until revenue covers it.

Exchange rate used: about ₦1,600 per $1 (Brela's 2026 pricing anchor). Check the day's rate before quoting. "$100" ≈ ₦160,000.

## The 15 services

Scores out of 5: **S** = speed to first payment, **F** = feasibility with what we have, **P** = profit per hour.

### 1. Small-business websites and landing pages, Lagos (naira)
- **Sell:** a 1–5 page mobile-first site with a WhatsApp order button, Google Maps location and a contact form. Hosted free in the client's own Cloudflare account.
- **Buyers:** Lagos restaurants, salons, caterers, boutiques, clinics, schools, logistics firms, churches, estate agents.
- **Demand evidence:** Nigerian agencies publish ₦100k–₦600k for 3–7 page sites and ₦80k–₦200k for landing pages (Etest, DPX, Brela, Nexoris price pages, 2026). Many small firms sell only through WhatsApp and Instagram. (That last point is an assumption to confirm in the first 10 conversations.)
- **Needs:** your coding skill, my drafting, free hosting. The client pays for their domain (₦1.5k–₦15k for .com.ng / .ng).
- **All free?** Yes.
- **First customer:** 1–2 weeks through your network and in-person visits.
- **Charge:** landing page ₦90k; 3-page site ₦150k; 5-page site ₦250k. 50% deposit.
- **Find customers:** your contacts, WhatsApp status, UNILAG/Yaba business clusters, Instagram businesses with no website (visit or DM), referrals.
- **Monthly potential:** 2–4 sites = ₦300k–₦800k (≈ $190–$500).
- **Score:** S5 F5 P3

### 2. Google Business Profile setup and optimisation, Lagos
- **Sell:** create or verify the profile and complete everything (categories, photos, hours, services, WhatsApp link, review QR card) so they appear on Google Maps.
- **Buyers:** the same Lagos SMEs. Many aren't on Maps at all.
- **Demand evidence:** US one-time GBP optimisation sells at $250–$899 (named providers, 2026). Nigerian agencies bundle it into SEO packages.
- **Needs:** the owner's Google account access; nothing else.
- **All free?** Yes.
- **First customer:** 1–2 weeks. It's an easy add-on to #1.
- **Charge:** ₦35k alone; ₦25k as an add-on to a website.
- **Find customers:** search Maps for "restaurant Yaba" and spot businesses with no photos, no hours or few reviews.
- **Monthly potential:** 4–8 profiles = ₦140k–₦280k.
- **Score:** S5 F5 P3

### 4. Landing page and website redesign, international (Fiverr gig)

- **Monthly potential:** $200–$1,000 once reviews exist.
- **Score:** S2 F5 P4

## Recommendation

  - **Lagos first:** fastest cash, ₦0 fees, 50% deposits, in-person trust.
  - **One portfolio, two markets:** the same portfolio sells on Fiverr in USD.

  - **Recurring income:** optional care plans.
- **SECONDARY: "Get Found" Google Business Profile + AI visibility.**
  - **Locally:** an add-on that lifts each sale by ₦25k–₦35k.

**First $100 = one 3-page website deposit + balance (₦150k), or one landing page (₦90k) + one GBP setup (₦35k).** It's realistic within 2–3 weeks if you make 20–30 real conversations happen. No outcome is guaranteed.
