# Customer Outreach: zero-capital (DRAFTS, nothing sent)

Channels that cost nothing: your existing contacts, WhatsApp status, Instagram DMs, walking into businesses, LinkedIn posts, and email to published business addresses. **No ads, no bulk messaging, no purchased lists.** Personalise every message. Stop when anyone says no.

## 1. Warm network: WhatsApp status / broadcast to people who saved your number
> 🚀 I'm now building websites and Google Maps listings for small businesses.
> ✅ Mobile-friendly site with WhatsApp order button
> ✅ Show up on Google Maps
> ✅ No monthly hosting fees
> From ₦90,000. Sites live in 5–7 days. Know a business that needs this? Send them my way, or DM me. I'll show you examples.

## 2. Warm network: personal message to someone who owns or knows a business
> Hi {{name}}, hope you're well! Quick one: I've started building websites and Google Maps profiles for small businesses. I noticed {{their business / their friend's business}} doesn't have {{a website / a Google Maps listing with photos}} yet. I'd love to build one for you: {{price}}, half upfront, live in a week. Can I send you a sample I made?

## 3. Instagram DM to a business with no website (check their bio first)
> Hi {{business}}! Love your {{specific product or post}}. I build simple websites for Lagos businesses so customers can see your {{menu/prices/catalogue}}, find you on Google and order on WhatsApp in one tap. Here's a sample: {{portfolio link}}. Would a site like this help you get more orders? Happy to send prices.

## 4. Walk-in script (Yaba, Akoka, Surulere, Ikeja shops)
1. "Good afternoon, I'm {{name}}. I help businesses around here show up on Google Maps."
2. Show your phone: their business on Google Maps (or missing from it), then a competitor that has photos and reviews.
3. "I can set this up for you today: profile, photos, hours, WhatsApp link. ₦35,000, and you only pay half now."
4. Leave a card or your WhatsApp number. Follow up in 2 days.

## 5. Google Maps gap message (businesses with an incomplete profile)
> Hello {{business}}, I searched "{{service}} near {{area}}" on Google Maps. Your listing {{has no photos / no opening hours / no phone number}}, while {{competitor}} shows {{x reviews and photos}}. I fix this for businesses for ₦35,000 (₦25,000 with a website). Want me to show you what yours would look like?

## 6. International (US prospects from `data/prospects.csv`): manual audit hook
Use the email in `docs/outreach.md`, filled only from a real manual check (ChatGPT/Perplexity apps, and Google through a free US VPN). Offer the $49 audit or the free summary. Send only after approval.

## Follow-up rhythm
- Day 2: "Did you get a chance to look at the sample?"
- Day 5: one useful tip, e.g. "Adding opening hours to your Google profile takes 2 minutes."
- Day 10: last message.

Log every contact in the Notion CRM (or `data/pipeline.csv`).
