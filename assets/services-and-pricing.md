# Services and Pricing

Payment rules for every service:
- **50% deposit before work starts, balance before handover.** Websites are handed over (client account access, domain connection) only after full payment.
- Free revisions are limited and stated in writing.
- Clients own everything: their domain, their Cloudflare/Google accounts, their content.
- We never put client sites in our own paid accounts and never pay for a client's tools.
- No guarantees of rankings, Google placement or AI recommendations. We guarantee **deliverables and deadlines.**
