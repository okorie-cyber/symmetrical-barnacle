# Recurring Income: build once, look after them monthly

**Why:** a one-off website pays once. A ₦35,000/month plan across 20 clients is ₦700,000 every month, and it compounds. Every website quote includes a recurring plan option, and every delivery ends with the offer.

## Market check (published prices, Nov 2026)
- **Nigerian care plans:** ₦25,000–₦75,000/month (Musskart). Atucore ₦35,000–₦150,000/month including hosting; Purs & Gold ₦80,000+/month with social media.
- **US basic care plans:** $50–$100/month. Done-for-you Google Business Profile management: $99–$400/month per location.

Our prices sit at the low-to-middle of the Lagos range. That's right for a new studio, and the margin is high because hosting is free and the client pays for third-party services directly.

## Rules
- **Month to month.** Cancel any time; the client keeps their site, domain and accounts.
- **The client pays third parties directly:** domain renewals, any paid tool, and WhatsApp Business **API** fees (Meta charges per conversation). We only use the **free** WhatsApp Business app until a client needs and pays for the API.
- **Scope is written down.** Extra work is quoted separately: ₦10,000 per extra page edit block, or $25.
- **No promises of rankings or sales.** We report what we did and what Google's own numbers show.
